\chapter{Container Binary Schema Specification}
\label{app:schema}

This appendix specifies the byte-level binary layout of the .tfc container versions 3, 4, and 5.

\section{Version 3 and 4 Segment Stream Layout}
In versions 3 and 4, the compressed payload for each color channel is arranged sequentially as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Packed Mode Map Header:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item 4 bytes: Uncompressed length of RLE mode stream $L_{\text{rle}}$.
        \item $L_{\text{rle}}$ bytes: Run-length encoded stream of 2-bit packed mode tokens.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Quantized Intercept Stream ($A$):}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Array of uint8 values corresponding to all units where $M(x, y) \in \{\textsc{Const}, \textsc{Linear}\}$.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Quantized Slope Stream ($B$):}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Array of signed int16 values ($2$ bytes each, little-endian) corresponding to units where $M(x, y) = \textsc{Linear}$.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Raw Fallback Sample Stream ($F$):}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Block of $T \times N_{\text{raw}}$ uncompressed uint8 samples for all units where $M(x, y) = \textsc{Raw}$.
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\section{Version 5 Chunked Record Framing}
A version 5 .tfc stream consists of a master header followed by independent chunk blocks:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\caption{Version 5 Binary Chunk Framing Syntax.}
\label{tab:v5_syntax}
\begin{tabular}{llp{7cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Byte Offset} & \textbf{Data Type} & \textbf{Field Description} \\
\midrule
$0 \dots 3$ & ASCII \texttt{"TFC5"} & Container magic bytes. \\
$4 \dots 7$ & uint32 (big-endian) & Master JSON header length ($L_{\text{json}}$). \\
$8 \dots 8 + L_{\text{json}} - 1$ & UTF-8 JSON & Video dimensions, rational frame rate, and audio parameters. \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{3}{c}{\textit{Repeated for each Video Chunk $k = 0, \dots, N_{\text{chunks}}-1$}} \\
\midrule
$0 \dots 3$ & uint32 & Chunk payload length in bytes ($S_{\text{chunk}}$). \\
$4 \dots 7$ & uint32 & CRC32 integrity checksum of chunk payload. \\
$8 \dots 9$ & uint16 & Number of video frames in this chunk ($T_{\text{chunk}}$). \\
$10 \dots 10 + S_{\text{chunk}} - 1$ & Binary Payload & Compressed planes (R, G, B) with RLE mode maps and quantized parameters. \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{3}{c}{\textit{Optional Audio Stream Interleaving}} \\
\midrule
$0 \dots 3$ & ASCII \texttt{"TFCA"} & Audio packet identifier tag. \\
$4 \dots 7$ & uint32 & Audio payload length ($S_{\text{audio}}$). \\
$8 \dots 8 + S_{\text{audio}} - 1$ & Compressed Audio & Raw audio packets (AAC/Opus) for corresponding time window. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
